What I do

Six ways I close the gap between the plan and the work.

I don't advise, deck, or workshop. I embed, run the operating cadence alongside your team, and hand it back when you can run it yourselves.

01

Execution diagnostic

I map your portfolio against the strategy and name the few initiatives that are real — and the ones quietly burning capacity.

02

Strategy to operating plan

I turn a multi-year strategy into fiscal operating plans teams can deliver against — the work I led for a $50B bank's technology and engineering org.

03

Embedded delivery leadership

I stand up and run delivery teams in-house — 27 people across consumer banking, core platforms, and crisis response — then hand the cadence back.

04

M&A integration

I integrate acquisitions end to end — people, technology, operations, synergies. Most recently a $200M cross-border deal that doubled the user base.

05

Agile transformation

I bootstrap teams from zero to shipping — an organization's first Agile team, trained and delivering inside 10 weeks.

06

Operating-model evolution

I rebuild how the work flows — shared services, portfolio management, and forecasting that makes the next budget honest.
